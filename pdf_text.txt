1. About the Yoga Program 
Sensationz Media & Arts offers Online Live Interactive Yoga Classes for beginners, intermediate 
learners, and experienced practitioners. 
Classes are conducted online through the Sensationz App, enabling students to join from anywhere 
in India or around the world. 
The physical studio is located in Rohini, Delhi, while the Yoga program is currently available online 
only.

Organization: Sensationz Media & Arts
